# Product Marketing Context: Snipra

## Product Overview
Snipra is a Swedish AI-SDR and outbound automation SaaS for B2B companies. It discovers relevant companies, detects public buying signals, analyzes company context, generates personalized Swedish sales emails, plans follow-ups and helps teams book more meetings.

## Target Audience
Swedish B2B consultancies, agencies, SaaS companies and service businesses selling to other companies in Sweden. Ideal users have a clear offer but too little time for manual prospecting, research and thoughtful follow-up.

## Personas
- Founder/CEO: wants predictable meetings without hiring a full SDR team.
- Sales manager: wants better prioritization, less generic outreach and clearer reporting.
- Marketing manager: wants messaging consistency and proof that campaigns are relevant.
- Operator: wants clean workflows, suppression, mailbox health and auditability.

## Positioning
Snipra replaces manual cold-email work with an embedded AI sales assistant built for Swedish B2B tone, signal-based timing and practical outbound workflows.

## Differentiation
- Swedish-first tone and locale.
- Signal-driven outreach instead of list blasting.
- Embedded assistant that controls workflows, not a separate chatbot.
- Clear LinkedIn and enrichment guardrails.
- Persistent business context reused across discovery, analysis, emails and reporting.

## Voice
Professional, calm, specific, human and credible. Avoid spam tone, exaggerated claims, clickbait and generic AI phrasing.

## Proof Themes
- More relevant first touches.
- Less research time per campaign.
- Better timing through signals.
- Safer sending through suppression, mailbox health and stop-on-reply logic.
